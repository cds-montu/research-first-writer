# 6. Research brief

```
Intent:         informational (how-to)
Target length:  1,500 words. The baselines' median is about 2,400, but much of that is promotion and repetition;
                the gap is accuracy, not length.
Baselines:      aioseo (benchmark) · sproutworth (freshest) · seojuice (contrarian) · magnet (gap)
Sensitive topic: no
```

## Differentiators (none of the baselines offers these)
1. **A crawler map from the engines' own docs**: search crawler vs training crawler vs user-triggered fetcher, for each engine. *gap*
2. **Myth check against primary sources**: Google-Extended, schema, llms.txt and the "41%" figure, each corrected with a quote. *gap*
3. **What the GEO study measured and didn't**: a simulated engine, GPT-3.5 judging, top-5 Google sources. *angle*
4. **A 30-minute checklist** a reader can run today. *format*

## Reader questions
- Do I need to rank on Google to be cited by ChatGPT? (high)
- Does blocking GPTBot remove me from ChatGPT search? (high)
- Is llms.txt worth adding? (high)
- How long until changes show up? (medium)
- How do I check whether I'm being cited? (medium)

## Terms
- **Fan-out:** the engine runs several searches for one prompt, then picks passages from the results.
- **Search crawler vs training crawler:** one indexes pages to cite them in answers; the other collects text to train models.

## Pitfalls
- Treating Google-Extended as the switch for AI Overviews.
- Treating vendor correlation studies as causal facts.
- Unsourced timelines ("4 to 8 weeks").
- Reporting 41% as the effect of adding statistics.
